1. PROJECT BACKGROUND
==================================================

PROJECT NAME: LASTMILE AI

TAGLINE: Every student deserves a way to reach their classroom.

PROJECT CATEGORY:
AI-powered rural transportation coordination and optimization platform.

PROBLEM:

Many rural students in India depend on a limited number of government buses to travel to schools and colleges.

Transportation becomes unreliable when:
- Buses are unavailable.
- Vehicles break down.
- Roads become inaccessible.
- Certain routes are disrupted.
- A bus has more students than available seats.
- Multiple villages need transport simultaneously.
- Some routes have excess capacity while others have unmet demand.
- Students do not know what alternative authorized transport is available.

Existing transportation resources are limited and poorly coordinated.

OUR SOLUTION:

LASTMILE AI is an intelligent transportation decision-support platform that uses AI-assisted reasoning, demand estimation, vehicle allocation, route optimization, and transparent recommendations to improve the utilization of existing rural transport resources.

The application should allow a transportation administrator to:
1. Understand transportation demand across villages.
2. Monitor the available approved vehicles.
3. Identify routes where demand exceeds capacity.
4. Forecast upcoming transportation requirements.
5. Optimize vehicle and seat allocation.
6. Simulate breakdowns and road closures.
7. Recommend alternative authorized transportation.
8. Understand why the AI recommends a particular allocation.
9. Generate an actionable transportation plan.

The transportation network must:
- Immediately mark both buses unavailable.
- Remove their capacities from usable resources.
- Identify their previously assigned students.
- Reallocate affected students.
- Recalculate available transportation capacity.
- Prevent unavailable buses from appearing in new assignments.

EVENT 2 — STUDENT DEMAND INCREASES BY 40%

Transportation demand suddenly increases by 40%.

The system must:
- Recalculate demand for every village.
- Update total student demand.
- Update capacity requirements.
- Update route-level demand.
- Identify the most affected villages.
- Calculate unmet transportation demand.

Apply the increase to the current total demand, using a
consistent integer allocation method so the sum remains
exactly equal to the rounded-up total.

EVENT 3 — A MAJOR ROAD IS BLOCKED BY FLOODING

A major road connecting two villages becomes inaccessible.

The system must:
- Mark that road as CLOSED.
- Remove it from all eligible route calculations.
- Identify affected routes.
- Find alternative paths through the remaining road network.
- Calculate detour travel time.
- Highlight longer journeys.
- Recalculate student arrival estimates.

EVENT 4 — EXAMINATIONS ARE STARTING WITHIN TWO HOURS

Several students have important examinations within the
next 120 minutes.

The system must:
- Identify examination-bound student groups.
- Record their examination start times.
- Calculate the latest feasible arrival time.
- Prioritize examination-bound students.

- Minimize the number of exam students unable to arrive
